## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional resource hub crafted specifically for educators, instructional designers, and educational leaders. It reconciles academic rigor with modern pedagogical dynamism. The visual tone projects prestige, trust, and structured clarity, ensuring high cognitive ease when educators evaluate and adopt classroom technologies.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Institutionalism** with **Vibrant Editorial Tech**:
- **Structured Precision**: Slate-grounded hierarchy, meticulous spacing intervals, and sharp borders cultivate immediate institutional credibility.
- **Pedagogical Energy**: Calculated, vibrant accents—interactive violet, energetic emerald, and digital cobalt—demarcate distinct instructional categories (gamification, assessment, AI workflows) without compromising professional gravity.
- **Clarity and Focus**: Minimal visual clutter ensures high information density remains digestible across complex comparison matrices, software walkthroughs, and curriculum planning templates.

## Colors

The palette leverages deep, slate-grounded neutrals to anchor institutional prestige, accompanied by vivid chromatic accents that categorize educational software ecosystems.

- **Primary (`#1E293B`)**: The deep slate backbone used for dominant structural frames, authoritative typography, persistent navigation bars, and primary interactive surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6`)**: Digital cobalt representing pedagogy and core tools; drives standard actions, primary hyperlinks, and selected filter states.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Vitality emerald highlighting practical classroom readiness, free-tier verifications, validated pedagogical impact badges, and active success states.
- **Accent Purple (`#8B5CF6`)**: Applied to cutting-edge gamification tags, automated evaluation modules, and AI instructional assistants.
- **Neutral Core (`#64748B`)**: Muted slate used for secondary metadata, subtle borders, inactive tab labels, and auxiliary text. Light canvas layers rest on pristine off-whites (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`) to mitigate eye fatigue during extended planning sessions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the geometric clarity of **Outfit** with the structural legibility of **Manrope**.

- **Display & Headings (Outfit)**: Pure geometric curves deliver authority and clear structure to educational modules, guide titles, and matrix headers. Tight letter-spacing maintains cohesion at scale.
- **Body & Controls (Manrope)**: Engineered for long-form readability, high contrast, and rapid information scanning. Generous open counters and x-height maintain distinct letterforms across dense evaluation rubrics and software configuration walkthroughs.
- **Multilingual Harmony**: When rendering Thai scripts alongside English technical terms, line heights are adjusted dynamically by `+10%` to preserve vertical clearance for loophead ascenders and tone marks without line collision.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an **8px linear scale** integrated into a 12-column responsive layout:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid, `margin-lg: 3rem`, `gutter-lg: 2rem`, maximum container width of `1280px`. Suited for dual-pane app comparisons, filterable matrices, and side-nav curation guides.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Tool sidebars fold into sticky, horizontal chip filters.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column fluid layout, `margin-sm: 1rem`, `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Comparison charts pivot to swipeable cards with fixed left headers.
- **Rhythm Rules**: Outer layout margins (`margin-*`) isolate the canvas frame, grid gaps (`gutter-*`) regulate column relations, and component spaces (`space-*`) dictate internal padding and card groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through crisp surface planes and diffuse ambient slate shadows, avoiding muddy or distracting drop-shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Neutral tint (`#F8FAFC`). Provides a balanced, glare-reducing workspace backdrop.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Shells)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a fine 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Flat on resting state; gains depth on hover via a soft slate shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Flyouts & Dropdown Filters)**: Raised pure white plane with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and a directional drop shadow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)`).
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals & Focus Walkthroughs)**: Elevated overlay flanked by an 80% opacity `#0F172A` backdrop blur (`8px`), centered cleanly in the field of view.

## Shapes

The interface implements **Roundedness Tier 2**:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges)**: Bound by an exact `0.5rem` (8px) radius. This provides modern approachability while retaining institutional precision.
- **Composite Containers (Cards, Modals, Comparison Matrices)**: Bound by `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to define self-contained content units.
- **Featured Hero Cards & Callout Panels**: Bound by `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for distinct visual focus.
- **Pills & Status Indicators**: Full-radius circular caps reserved solely for categorical metadata chips, live status indicators, and numeric count tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Buttons**: Deep slate (`#1E293B`) solid background, pure white text, `0.5rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.25rem` padding. Subtle hover shifts to `#334155`.
- **Secondary Buttons**: Transparent base enclosed with a 1.5px border (`#CBD5E1`), text set in `#1E293B`. Active hover fills to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Category Action Buttons**: Highlighted in secondary cobalt (`#3B82F6`) or tertiary emerald (`#10B981`) exclusively for tool launching and trial access.

### Chips & Taxonomies
- **Tool Taxonomy Chips**: Light tinted fills (e.g., `#EDE9FE` for Violet/AI, `#ECFDF5` for Emerald/Assessments, `#EFF6FF` for Cobalt/LMS), text in matching saturated ink, bounded by `0.5rem` radius or pill curvature. Includes a 12px leading vector icon.
- **Filter Chips**: Resting state in `#F1F5F9` neutral with a 1px slate-line border. Selected state inverts to `#1E293B` slate fill with white text.

### Cards & Grid Showcase
- **Tool Showcase Card**: Level 1 white surface with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners. Header houses the tool monogram/logo alongside category tags. Body features a 3-line truncated pedagogical summary. Footer includes a 2-column split: feature checklist icons (left) and direct access link (right).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Resource Search Bar**: Prominent `0.75rem` vertical padding, `rounded-lg` profile, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, anchored by a leading search icon. Focus ring applies a 3px outline in `#3B82F6` with 20% alpha.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Matrix Selectors
- **Selection Controls**: 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with a 1.5px stroke in `#94A3B8`. Selected state fills with `#3B82F6` and features a crisp white icon.

### Comparison Matrix Table
- **Feature Matrix**: Alternating header row in `#F8FAFC` with bold Outfit headings. Sticky left tool column with right-pinned evaluation checkmarks (`#10B981`) and missing feature indicators (`#CBD5E1`).
